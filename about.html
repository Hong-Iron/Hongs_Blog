Leading a 200-member student chapter

President of Chung-Ang Univ. CCC, August 2025 to August 2026. What I did in that year:

  • Wrote the direction down. The vision, the goals for each semester, and what each officer's role is for, as documents the leaders' meetings worked from (about 30 small-group leaders).
  • Rebuilt how the team keeps records. Restructured the shared drive, wrote the record-keeping guide, and built a dashboard showing upcoming events, outreach status and the days left in the term.
  • Developed a ministry management tool. Roster and org chart, an event flow from proposal to cue sheet to settlement, schedule and meetings, attendance. It is there so the next leadership team can plan in one place.
  • Planned events. Homecoming day, the club fair manual and script, and the spring welcome party that brought 42 newcomers.
  • Spoke about it. Presented the chapter's newcomer work as a case study at the leaders' retreat, and wrote and taught an elective session, "Debugging a Community", at the 2026 summer retreat.
  • Represented the club to the university. Sat on the club representatives' council, handled re-registration, and traced a missing subsidy until it was paid.

What the chapter did together in that year: newcomer drop-off fell from 12.4% to 2.4%, 49 of 56 new students (87.5%) went through the newcomer small groups, and more than 110 people came to the summer retreat.
